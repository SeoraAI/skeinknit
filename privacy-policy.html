Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Skein ("we," "our," or "the app") is a knitting toolkit developed by Nice People Collective LLC. This policy describes how we handle your information.

The short version

Your knitting content is saved on your device. When iCloud is available for Skein, supported content also syncs through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud account. You do not need a separate Skein account. We use Mixpanel for usage analytics, which you can turn off in the app's Settings.

Your content and iCloud

Skein saves projects, patterns, imported PDFs, selected photos, yarn stash entries, pattern drafts, stitch charts, favorites, and designer details locally. Supported records and their attached files can sync between your devices using the same iCloud account. This includes designer details you choose to enter, such as your name, bio, website, and reply-to email address.

Sync uses your private CloudKit database, not a public pattern library or a content server operated by us. Availability depends on your iCloud account, device settings, connection, and available storage. Some preferences and tools, including PDF reading markers and local reminder schedules, remain device-local.

Removing the app is not the same as deleting its iCloud data. Cloud copies may remain and return when you reinstall or use another device. Deletions made in the app can sync to your other devices when sync is available. You can manage iCloud storage through Apple's account and device settings. Keep separate copies of important original PDFs and exports.

Photos, camera, and imported files

You choose which photos and documents to add. Skein requests camera or photo-library permission when needed for a feature you choose, such as adding a pattern photo. You can decline or change these permissions in your device's Settings. Selected files and photos are stored with your content and may sync through your private iCloud account; they are not sent to Mixpanel.

Analytics

Skein uses Mixpanel to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Mixpanel receives:

  • Usage events, such as opening screens, importing a pattern, counting rows, completing a grading, exporting a PDF, and purchase or restore outcomes; these may include feature categories, counts, and product identifiers
  • Standard app and device information, such as app version, device model, and operating system version
  • A randomly generated identifier that distinguishes one installation from another

That identifier is not your name, email address, or Apple ID, and we do not link it to your identity. It is, however, persistent for as long as the app is installed, so it is pseudonymous rather than strictly anonymous. We want to describe that accurately rather than overstate it.

We do not send your pattern text, PDFs, photos, project notes, measurements, or designer contact details to Mixpanel. Skein disables Mixpanel's IP-based location lookup. Network requests still involve an IP address as part of ordinary internet communication.

Usage sharing is on by default in release builds. To stop future analytics collection, turn off “Share anonymous usage data” in Skein's Settings. Despite the label, the installation identifier makes this data pseudonymous. Turning sharing off does not by itself erase events already received. Contact us with analytics privacy questions or deletion requests; do not send your private patterns or payment information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Skein offers optional Designer Pro subscriptions and a Lifetime purchase, processed entirely through Apple's App Store using Apple's StoreKit framework. We never see or receive your payment details. Subscription management, billing, and renewals are handled by Apple. We do not use any third-party subscription or payment service.

Submitting patterns by email

The Designer Workspace can prepare a submission pitch and open it in your own email app. You choose the recipient and you send it yourself from your own email account. We do not receive a copy, and we do not store the addresses you send to.

What we never do

We do not sell or rent your information or use it for advertising. The services described above process data to provide iCloud sync, purchases, and usage analytics. We do not include advertising networks in the app.

Children's privacy

Skein is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete items in Skein, manage camera and photo permissions in device Settings, turn off usage sharing in Skein's Settings, and manage iCloud storage through Apple. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove cloud copies or previously collected analytics. If you contact support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send so we can respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
