Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Skein ("we," "our," or "the app") is a knitting toolkit developed by Nice People Collective LLC. This policy describes how we handle your information.

The short version

Everything you create in Skein — your projects, patterns, yarn stash, gauge measurements, and grading calculations — is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not run servers that hold your content. The one exception is anonymous usage analytics, described below.

What stays on your device

The following is saved locally on your iPhone and is never transmitted to us:

  • Projects and row-tracking progress
  • Saved patterns and favorites
  • Yarn stash entries, including any photos you add
  • Gauge measurements and grading calculations
  • Pattern drafts written in the Designer Workspace
  • Designer details you enter for submissions — your name, bio, website or Ravelry link, and reply-to email address

Deleting the app removes all of this permanently. We keep no backup of it.

Analytics

Skein uses Mixpanel to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Mixpanel receives:

  • Events for a small number of actions, such as opening the app, completing a grading, exporting a PDF, viewing the subscription screen, and completing a purchase
  • Standard app and device information, such as app version, device model, and operating system version
  • A randomly generated identifier that distinguishes one installation from another

That identifier is not your name, email address, or Apple ID, and we do not link it to your identity. It is, however, persistent for as long as the app is installed, so it is pseudonymous rather than strictly anonymous. We want to describe that accurately rather than overstate it.

Mixpanel never receives the content you create — no patterns, projects, stash entries, photos, measurements, or designer details. Deleting the app resets the identifier.

Subscriptions

Skein offers an optional subscription, Designer Pro, processed entirely through Apple's App Store using Apple's StoreKit framework. We never see or receive your payment details. Subscription management, billing, and renewals are handled by Apple. We do not use any third-party subscription or payment service.

Submitting patterns by email

The Designer Workspace can prepare a submission pitch and open it in your own email app. You choose the recipient and you send it yourself from your own email account. We do not receive a copy, and we do not store the addresses you send to.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or share your information. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not include advertising networks in the app, and we do not build profiles about you.

Children's privacy

Skein is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Because your content lives on your device, you control it entirely. You can delete individual items inside the app, or remove everything by deleting the app. If you would like us to answer questions about analytics or request that we stop processing usage data associated with your installation, email us at the address below and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
